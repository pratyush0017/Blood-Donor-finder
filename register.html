Basic Donor Eligibility

  • Age between 18 and 65 years and in good general health.
  • Minimum weight of 50 kg (110 lbs) recommended by standard blood banks.
  • At least 90 days since your last whole-blood donation.
  • Stay well hydrated and eat a balanced meal before donating.

Privacy & Demo Notice

This registration form stores demonstration records exclusively in your browser's localStorage. Public search cards never reveal personal phone numbers or street addresses.
